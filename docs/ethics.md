# Ethics, privacy and research integrity

All profiles, departments and publications are locally authored synthetic data. No scraping, private university resources, institutional branding, analytics, accounts or personal researcher records are used. Synthetic names may coincidentally resemble real people; no identity claim is intended.

Cosine similarity is a mathematical relationship between text vectors. It is not research quality, merit, employability, impact or verified topical relevance. Researchers should not be judged by these demonstration rankings. A user must read the profile and use research judgement.

The pretrained embedding model can encode historical, cultural and linguistic biases. English-centric retrieval and sparse profile descriptions can systematically exclude people or topics. Synthetic examples do not demonstrate fairness. Model upgrades require reproducible retrieval evaluation and ideally stakeholder review. Related concepts are separately scored suggestions, not a faithful explanation of a neural model.

Queries are processed locally in the backend and are not transmitted to a hosted AI API. They appear in URLs/browser history and briefly in the bounded process cache. Do not enter confidential text. Application access logs are disabled, but deployment operators must review external logging. Initial dependency and model installation contacts public registries; those providers have their own request logging policies.

Each stored vector records the model name/revision, content hash and generation time. The dataset version and deterministic generators support reproducibility. Before replacing it with public or private real-world data, define provenance, lawful basis, consent where appropriate, correction/deletion processes, retention and transparent opt-out behaviour. Do not simply enable scraping.
